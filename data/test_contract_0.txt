Contract 0 — Sample research questions

For each question, cite the relevant operative clauses and identify any qualifications, exceptions, or unavailable referenced materials.

1. What consideration do Acacia stockholders receive for their shares in the merger, and how does the agreement address shares that receive different treatment?

2. What happens to outstanding company options, restricted stock units, and performance stock units when the merger takes effect?

3. What conditions must all parties satisfy before the merger can close?

4. What additional closing conditions apply specifically to Cisco and Amarone Acquisition Corp.?

5. What additional closing conditions apply specifically to Acacia?

6. What obligations do the parties have to obtain regulatory approvals, and what limitations or qualifications apply to those obligations?

7. Under what circumstances can either party terminate the merger agreement, including if the merger has not closed by the applicable outside date?

8. When is a termination fee payable, who must pay it, and what conditions trigger it?

9. What restrictions apply to Acacia soliciting or responding to competing acquisition proposals, and when may its board change its recommendation?

10. What protections does the agreement provide to Acacia's directors and officers after the merger, and how long do those protections last?
